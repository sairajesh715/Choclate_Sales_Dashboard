# Get a Shareable Link in 4 Steps

## Go to railway.app

**Step 1** — Open https://railway.app → click **Start a New Project** → **Login with GitHub**

**Step 2** — Click **"Deploy from GitHub repo"** → select **Choclate_Sales_Dashboard**
- Railway will start deploying automatically

**Step 3** — Click **"+ New"** (top right of your project) → **Database** → **Add MySQL**
- Wait ~30 seconds for it to turn green
- The app will automatically connect and load all the data (no import needed!)

**Step 4** — Click on your app service → **Settings tab** → under **Domains** click **"Generate Domain"**
- You'll get a link like: `https://choclate-sales-dashboard.up.railway.app`
- **Copy and share this link on WhatsApp, email, anywhere!**

---

That's it. The database fills itself automatically on first run.
